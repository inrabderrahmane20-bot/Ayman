# Competitor and market landscape

**Research date:** 2026-10-26
**Owner of this file:** AYMAN project
**Status:** qualitative landscape only. **No competitor traffic, revenue, pricing, ranking, backlink, ad-spend, conversion or audience figure is reported in this document.**

## 1. Why there are no competitor metrics here

No Ahrefs, Semrush, SimilarWeb, Moz, SpyFu, Meta Ad Library export, Google Keyword Planner, Search Console or analytics property was available. Manual search-result checks were performed on 2026-10-26 for the three target markets, but a manual check cannot produce reliable traffic, keyword-overlap or revenue estimates.

Accordingly, every metric field in this document is **unavailable**. Nothing has been estimated, inferred from intuition, or copied from a blog post. If the owner later buys a data tool, the scorecard in section 7 is the place to record real values with a date and source.

## 2. Markets in scope

| Market | Language priority | Buyer context |
|---|---|---|
| Morocco | French and Moroccan Arabic first; English secondary | Price-sensitive; mixed-language search; local gyms and independent coaches compete for attention |
| Saudi Arabia | Arabic first; English secondary | Higher willingness to pay for private coaching; strong preference for Arabic-language trust signals |
| Qatar | Arabic and English | Smaller market; large expatriate segment; English and Arabic both commercially relevant |
| Worldwide online | English first; French and Arabic | Time-zone and language fit matter more than geography |

The business is online-only from Morocco. No physical gym, clinic or local presence is claimed anywhere on the site. This removes the need to compete on local-pack listings, but also removes “near me” intent.

## 5. AYMAN’s defensible position

These are positioning choices supported by the actual build, not performance claims:

1. **Three real languages, not machine-translated pages.** English, French and Arabic are written as separate copy, with Arabic RTL and an Arabic typeface.
2. **No app claim.** Delivery is written plans, documents and messaging. This is stated plainly on pricing and in the FAQ.
3. **Transparent scope and exclusions.** Medical care, physiotherapy, diagnostic tests and registered-dietitian services are explicitly excluded.
4. **Honest proof.** Unverifiable transformations, ratings, country counts and scarcity cues were removed. The only retained scale figure is the owner-reported “220+ clients coached”.
5. **Application before payment.** No checkout, no subscription trap; fit is checked first.
6. **Clear pricing in USD.** $99 / $169 / $249 per month, billed monthly, no minimum term, cancellation before renewal.
7. **Local relevance without false local presence.** Morocco-based, serving Morocco, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and worldwide online; no fake address or local-pack claim.
8. **A real conversion path.** Validated server-side lead intake with honest delivered / not-delivered states and WhatsApp/email fallback.

Positioning statement:

> Online fitness and nutrition coaching in English, French and Arabic. A written plan, scheduled reviews and direct support — with the scope, limits and price stated before you apply.

## 6. What not to do

- Do not claim “best coach”, “#1”, “top-rated” or “most trusted” without a verifiable source.
- Do not compare on price against a named competitor unless their price was checked on a dated screenshot.
- Do not copy competitor testimonials, transformation photos or programme names.
- Do not claim a competitor’s weakness (“their coaches are unqualified”, “their plans don’t work”) without evidence.
- Do not buy or imply reviews, ratings or awards.
- Do not present `coachayman.com` as an affiliated brand.
