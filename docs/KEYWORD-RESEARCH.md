# AYMAN — Keyword and market research

**Research date:** 2026-10-26.
**Data access:** no Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, Bing Webmaster or Search Console access was available. Therefore **no search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, backlink count, competitor traffic or ranking position is reported**. Every keyword below is a hypothesis derived from the offer, page structure and visible search-result patterns — not a measured demand figure.

This file exists so the content plan is auditable and so the missing data is explicit rather than silently filled with invented numbers.

## 1. Markets and languages

| Market | Primary language | Secondary | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morocco | French and Moroccan Arabic | English | Mixed-language search behaviour; French is common for coaching, Arabic for nutrition and body-composition topics |
| Saudi Arabia | Arabic | English | Arabic-first commercial intent; English used for some fitness terms |
| Qatar | Arabic | English | Smaller market, high expatriate share; English and Arabic both relevant |
| Worldwide online | English | French, Arabic | Time-zone and language fit rather than geography |

The site serves all four from Morocco, entirely online. No local clinic, gym or in-person service is claimed.

## 2. Method

1. Read the existing site and brand profile to identify the actual services, prices and constraints.
2. Reviewed the visible search results for the priority markets to identify the **types** of results present, language mix and page formats (qualitative only; no positions or volumes recorded).
3. Built keyword clusters around the four services, the buying stage and the objections the copy already answers.
4. Mapped each cluster to a page that can satisfy it without inventing proof.
5. Marked every metric as unavailable until a tool or first-party property is connected.

## 3. Search-result observations (qualitative)

Across the Morocco, Saudi and Qatar checks:

- Results mix coaching businesses, gyms, directories, marketplaces and social profiles.
- Arabic queries often return Arabic pages, but many results are thin or machine-translated.
- French queries for Morocco return a mix of local coaches, gyms and generic programme pages.
- “Personal protection” queries in Morocco return security companies and directories, not coaching businesses. This supports keeping that service separate and gated.
- Brand searches are ambiguous: `coachayman.com` is a **different live business** (executive/capability coaching). The site must not compete for or canonicalize to that brand.
- No result set was audited with a rank tracker, so no “we rank” or “competitor ranks” statement is made.

## 6. Prioritisation without volume data

Because volumes and difficulty are unavailable, priority is based on business value and existing page readiness:

1. **P0:** home, coaching index, pricing, apply, four service pages, contact.
2. **P1:** results policy, about, FAQ, privacy/terms/cookies.
3. **P2:** localized variants of every P0/P1 page (already built).
4. **P3:** verified case studies, protection launch — both blocked on owner evidence.

This is an editorial judgment, not a forecast of traffic.

## 7. What must be measured before scaling content

- Impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query and page (Search Console).
- Which locale and service page produce qualified applications (GA4 + lead review).
- Brand vs non-brand split.
- Query language mix in Morocco, Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
- Whether fallback contact is common (delivery problem vs demand problem).
- Keyword volumes and difficulty from a paid tool, if the owner subscribes.

Until those exist, adding dozens of speculative articles would be content volume without evidence.
